RamaPay is an easy to use and secure wallet for the Ramestta blockchain with native RAMA, ERC20, ERC721 and ERC875 support. RamaPay supports Ramestta mainnet and other Ethereum-compatible networks.

Focus on Defi:
- Shows comprehensive breakdown on Token transactions & swaps.
- Automatic discovery of tokens.
- Tickers for all main tokens.

Coming soon:
- Improved Defi interaction; full performance history on per-token and per-account basis.
- Easy interaction with tokens using TokenScript.

RamaPay has comprehensive crypto management for Ramestta and EVM chains. The real power of blockchain is in being able to interact with tokens in an intuitive and simple way. RamaPay is the wallet for the Ramestta ecosystem - designed for token control and seamless DApp interaction.

Version 3.91 brings improved dark mode support and enhanced security.
